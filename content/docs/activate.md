Most of MotionQL is free and needs no key at all. A **Pro** key unlocks the remaining features, and every registered user gets one **free for 12 months**.

## What Pro unlocks

| Edition | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Free** (no key) | Connections, query bar, visual query builder, IntelliShell, aggregation editor, SQL Query, Query Code, import and export, dump and restore, Compare and Sync, schema analysis, indexes, profiler, dashboards and the AI assistant with your own key |
| **Pro** | Everything in Free, plus **SQL Migration**, **Data Masking**, the **Tasks scheduler** and **MongoDB Atlas** management |

The first time you start MotionQL, a 14-day trial of the Pro features begins automatically. Activate your free key any time before or after it ends.

## Get your free Pro key

1. Go to [motionql.com/register](/register) and create an account with your name, email and a password.
2. Open the confirmation email and click the link to verify your address.
3. Sign in and open your [account page](/account). Your **Pro license** is listed there with its expiry date. Click **Copy** to copy the key. It is a long string that starts with `MQL1.`

No credit card is needed.

## Activate the key in MotionQL

1. Open MotionQL and go to **Settings → License** (**Mod+,** opens Settings; **Mod** is Cmd on macOS and Ctrl on Windows).
2. Paste the key and click **Activate**.
3. The License page now shows **Pro**, your name, and the expiry date.

Activation happens **offline**. MotionQL checks the key's digital signature against a public key built into the app; nothing is sent anywhere, so it also works behind firewalls and on air-gapped machines.

## Renewing after 12 months

While the free launch offer runs, you can renew for another year with one click on your [account page](/account). Renewing issues a new key; paste it into **Settings → License** the same way.

If a key expires, the Pro features stop but MotionQL keeps working: it opens, connects and shows your data, every free feature still works, and nothing is deleted. You can still view and remove items you made with Pro features, such as saved tasks.

## Replacing a lost or leaked key

On your account page, **Reissue** creates a new key with the same end date and cancels the old one. MotionQL learns about cancelled keys from a signed list it downloads periodically, so a cancelled key stops working on machines that are online.

## Troubleshooting activation

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| "This is not a MotionQL license key." | Paste the complete key, starting with `MQL1.`, with no line breaks |
| "The license key signature is not valid …" | The key was changed while copying. Copy it again from your account page |
| "The license key is signed but its contents are not valid." | [Contact support](/support) |
| Clock-rollback warning | Your computer's clock moved backwards. Correct the date and time |
