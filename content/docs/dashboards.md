Dashboards put **charts**, **metrics**, **tables** and **notes** on a drag-and-resize grid. Every widget is backed by a live aggregation on your connection.

## Build a dashboard

1. Open **Dashboards** from the command palette and create a dashboard, or start from a built-in **template** or a **one-click layout**.
2. Add a widget from the widget library and write or pick the aggregation behind it.
3. Drag and resize widgets on the grid.
4. Add **shared filters** that apply to every widget, set **auto refresh**, and cap slow queries with **`maxTimeMS`**.

## The chart editor

The chart editor shows a **live preview** as you change it.

- **Chart types:** column, bar, stacked, line, area, pie, doughnut, scatter, radar and polar area.
- **Date grouping** from hour to year.
- **Series**, color **palettes**, **legends**, **axis titles**, **number formats** and **value labels**.

## Present and export

- **Present mode** shows the dashboard full screen.
- **Export** to PNG, a multi-page **PDF** (A4 or Letter, split between widgets), per-widget PNG or CSV, or the whole dashboard as **JSON**.

Save your own dashboards as templates. Dashboards are stored on your computer.
